# Halverson Works — the story

You are a **night inspector** sent to Halverson Works after the plant went dark
at **2:07 AM**. Fourteen night-shift workers; nobody answers the radio.

## What happened

- **Foreman Ellis** pushed the night shift past its limits to hit a 4,000-unit
  quota. He ordered the boiler pressure valves kept open, past the 140 PSI limit.
- At 02:07 Line 3 split. Workers began shutting valves; Ellis went down to the
  **boiler room** to override valve B himself.
- At 02:11 the main breaker tripped. The plant went dark and the automatic
  **gate seal** clamped shut: it only releases while the generator is running.
- The **electrician** pulled the generator fuses and hid them around the plant so
  Ellis could not restart the line and send more men into the boiler.
- The boiler hatch was **welded shut from the inside**. Ellis never came out.
  Workers started disappearing. Something now walks the plant, stops when
  watched, and stays on the shift forever. It is Ellis.

## The goal, and why it makes sense

| Objective | Why |
|---|---|
| Close pressure valves A, B, C | The generator will blow if the pipes are still over-pressured |
| Find the red, blue and yellow fuses | The electrician hid them; each fuse slot glows in the Electrical room |
| Collect 3 gas cans | The generator's fuel is kept in the Fuel Store |
| Start the generator | Restores power and releases the gate seal |
| Leave through the gate | Ellis: "It always opens… for the next shift." |

## The plant (north is the entrance, south is the exit)

## Notes (press E to read)

Lobby notice, Mara's unsent letter (locker room), Ellis's memo (break room),
pressure log (steam hall), electrician's note (electrical), crate scrawl (fuel
store), boiler room log, and Ellis's last log (generator hall).

## Editing the level

`scenes/level_map.tscn` is generated by `tools/gen_level.py`
(`python tools/gen_level.py`). Edit the script, re-run it, and keep the
spawn markers (`PlayerSpawn`, `EnemySpawn`, `GeneratorSpawn`, gas/fuse spawns,
`ValveSpawns`) — `main.gd` and `level_map.gd` depend on them.

## The ending

Reaching the open gate fades to black and cuts to `scenes/ending.tscn`: dawn at
the factory gate. The camera turns back to the plant, the windows go dark one by
one, the gate slams, the foreman stands in the gateway and the dead sign comes
back on: "NEXT SHIFT 22:00". A title card shows the run time and how many of the
eight notes were read. SPACE skips to the card.

## Building a release

Export templates for the editor version must be installed. From the project root:

```
godot --headless --path . --export-release "Windows Desktop" build/HalversonWorks.exe
```

The preset embeds the data pack, so `build/HalversonWorks.exe` is a single file
(about 135 MB) you can zip and share. `export_presets.cfg` holds the preset.

After changing the level, regenerate and re-bake its navigation mesh:

```
python tools/gen_level.py
godot --headless --path . res://tools/bake_navmesh.tscn
python tools/gen_level.py
```

## Enemy rules (what the player can learn)

- **Phases:** valves closed -> he also patrols the plant in the open; fuse box powered -> shorter cooldowns, faster, more patrols.
- **Light:** a lit flashlight shows you from much farther (and in a wider cone). Held on a chasing foreman for half a second it staggers him (7 s cooldown). Crouching in the dark shrinks how far he sees you.
- **Noise:** footsteps (sprinting most of all) shorten the wait before he next appears, and draw a patrolling foreman to the sound.
- **Hiding:** lockers (press Interact). If he saw you go in, he waits outside and opens the door after ~2.6 s. If not, he checks the spot he last saw you and gives up. While stalking he loses interest in a hidden player.
- **Fair play:** every chase starts with a roar and a ~1 s freeze, and the game over screen says what went wrong.
